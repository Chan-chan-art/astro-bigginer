## All about Arc de Triomphe

The Arc de Triomphe is a famous historic monument in Paris, France, that honors those who fought and died for the nation.

History and Design

Commission:Napoleon Bonaparte ordered the arch built in 1806 after his victory at the Battle of Austerlitz.

Architect: Jean-François-Thérèse Chalgrin designed the structure using neoclassical ideas inspired by Rome's

Arch of Titus.Construction: Building took 30 years due to political delays, finishing in 1836 under King 

Louis-Philippe.Dimensions: The monument stands 50 meters (164 feet) tall and 45 meters (148 feet) wide.
