## All about The Eiffle Tower

The Eiffel Tower is a famous wrought-iron landmark located on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France.

History and Construction

Built between 1887 and 1889 as the entrance gateway to the 1889 World's Fair (Exposition Universelle).

Named after the engineer Gustave Eiffel, whose company designed and built the structure.

Made from more than 18,000 iron pieces held together by 2.5 million rivets.

Saved from demolition in 1909 because of its value as a radio telegraph station.

Key Facts and Numbers

Stands at a height of 330 meters (1,083 feet) including the tip.

Weighs approximately 10,100 tons.

Features three levels for visitors, with restaurants on the first and second levels.

Changes height by up to 15 centimeters (6 inches) depending on the seasonal temperature.
